About the SSLG

Who we are and what we stand for

Mission

To serve as the voice of the students of Marcial O. Rañola Memorial School, promoting their welfare, rights, and overall development through active governance and meaningful programs.

Vision

A student government that embodies integrity, transparency, and excellence — empowering every learner to become a responsible and engaged member of society.

Core Values

Service · Integrity · Excellence · Unity · Accountability

Who We Are

The Supreme Secondary Learner Government

The Supreme Secondary Learner Government (SSLG) of Marcial O. Rañola Memorial School is the highest governing body of secondary students. It represents all learners in school activities, decision-making, and welfare concerns.

Established in accordance with DepEd guidelines, the SSLG serves as the official voice of the student body, coordinating with school administration to ensure a conducive and empowering learning environment.

Every year, qualified students are elected to serve as officers, each committed to upholding the rights and welfare of their fellow learners through programs, projects, and active representation.
